Common Queries

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our QR + NFC review card product and platform.

Is there a recurring monthly or annual subscription fee?

expand_more
No. Our product is sold on a straightforward one-time payment basis. You purchase the configured cards or stands, and your dedicated redirection link is maintained without monthly software subscriptions.

Does this violate Google's review policies?

expand_more
Our platform is designed to follow Google review policies. Google prohibits review gating (intercepting negative feedback or offering incentives for positive reviews). ReviewBridge displays a completely neutral invitation ("How was your experience with us?") and redirects every customer directly to your official Google Review page. Reviews are written and submitted independently by the customer.

Do customers need to install an app to use the cards?

expand_more
No app download is needed. Modern iPhones and Android devices have native NFC readers built in that automatically trigger the browser when tapped, as well as native QR code scanning directly through the default camera app.

What happens if my business Google review link changes?

expand_more
Because your card links to your unique ReviewBridge URL (/r/your-store), your destination Google review link can be updated on the central redirection platform without needing to reorder or reprogram the physical card.

Can you customize cards with our business branding?

expand_more
Yes. When ordering, we configure your review card with your business name and brand details so your customer experience feels cohesive from counter to screen.
